# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: **October 31, 2025**

Thank you for using **Notification History** ("we", "our", "us").  
This document explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Locally Stored Data
The app records and stores certain data **only on your device** for functionality, including:
- Status bar notifications  
- Toast messages  
- USSD and Class 0 (Flash SMS) messages  
- App installation, update, and uninstall events  
- Dialog messages from other apps or the system  

> None of this data is uploaded, shared, or transmitted externally.

### 1.2 Cloud Data (Firebase Firestore)
We use **Firebase Firestore** only for:
- Storing **app version** (for update and compatibility tracking)  
- Storing **feedback** you voluntarily submit within the app  

No personal, sensitive, or notification data is stored in Firebase.

---

## 2. How We Use the Data

We use the collected information to:
- Ensure the app works correctly across Android versions  
- Improve features based on feedback  
- Diagnose and fix bugs  

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

---

## 3. Permissions Used

| Permission | Purpose |
|-------------|----------|
| Accessibility Service | Detect USSD dialogs, toast messages, and Class 0 (Flash SMS). |
| Notification Access | Read and store notifications locally. |
| Run at Startup | Manage old or expired notification records. |
| Internet Access | Send feedback and app version info to Firebase Firestore. |

---

## 4. Data Security

- Notification data remains **only on your device**.  
- Firebase data (version + feedback) is stored securely using **Google Firebase** services.  
- No personal identifiers are collected or transmitted.  
- You may delete local data or uninstall the app at any time to remove all stored information.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

The app is **not designed for children under 13**.  
We do not knowingly collect or store any information from children.

---

## 6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.  
When updated, the “Last Updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

---

## 7. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use the **Feedback** option within the app.
